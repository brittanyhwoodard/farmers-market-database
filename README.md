<h2>Farmers Market Proposal (Minnesota area only)</h2>
<p>The Farmers Market will be a platform designed to connect Minnesota's local farmers with consumers using an online database to bring a modern experience to find their needs. The database will include the farmers’ market location, vendors with a specific item and they will be able to place online orders. We will as a group hope that we can show the following features:</p> 
<h2>Minnesota Market Video Link: </h2>
<p>https://youtu.be/eXTpyI51LO8</p>
<h3>Support Local Farmers</h3>
<p>It makes it easier for people to buy directly from small farmers, which helps local businesses thrive.</p> 
<h3>Boost the Local Economy</h3>
<p>Vendors get more visibility and can sell more products by using a simple online system.</p> 
<h3>Accessibility</h3>
<p>Busy customers can quickly find markets, browse products, and pre-order items without having to go to every market in person.</p>
<h4>1. Farmers Market Directory</h4>
<p>The Farmers Markets can be filtered by location, date, vendors, and specialty items.  If there are catalogs of the products and if Pre-ordering is available.</p>
<h4>2. Admin Dashboard</h4>
<p>Can manage their inventory, view orders, and update market attendance for Vendors.</p>
<h4>3. Payment actions</h4>
<p>Will be designed with payment options for online orders, show if vendors accept only cash or cash/card.</p>
<h4>4. Customer Experience</h4>
<p>Browse markets and vendors
  <br>
Search for products across all markets.
  <br>
Place pre-orders with quantity and price details.
  <br>
Select payment option (cash, card, or online transaction).
  <br>
Receive order confirmation and pickup details.</p>
<h4>5. Administration</h4>
<p>Admins can manage farmers market listings, vendor accounts, and customer issues.
  <br>
Ensure data consistency and system security</p>
